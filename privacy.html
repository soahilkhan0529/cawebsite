YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

SOHAIL FINCORP respects your privacy. This policy explains how information submitted through this website may be handled. This website is a general introduction to our services; it is not a secure client portal.

Information you share

If you use the contact form, you may provide your name, work email, business name, the service you are interested in and any message you choose to include. When you submit the form, these details are transmitted to and stored in our Supabase-hosted database so we can manage and respond to your enquiry. Your email application may also open with a copy of your note ready to send. Please do not include passwords, identity numbers, bank details or confidential financial documents.

How information may be used

Information you submit may be used to respond to your enquiry, understand your business requirements and communicate with you about relevant professional services. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and third-party services

This site does not set analytics or advertising cookies. The site may load typography from Google Fonts, which can receive technical request information from your browser. The contact form uses Supabase to receive and store submissions.

Data security and retention

We restrict access to contact submissions and retain correspondence only as needed to respond to enquiries, meet professional obligations and maintain appropriate business records. If we begin an engagement, we will explain the appropriate channels and terms for sharing records.

Your choices

You may contact us at hello@nivara.co.in with questions about information you have sent or to request its correction or deletion, subject to applicable record-keeping requirements.

Updates

We may update this notice as our website or practices change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
